### About me

I'm a full-stack developer from Spain. In 2024 I started [Zoometrics](https://zoo-metrics.com) with Diana Barrantes, a GovTech company that builds data infrastructure for managing urban biodiversity. City councils, associations, volunteers and vets use our tools to keep track of the animals they share their cities with.

I'm still hands-on with product, architecture, data and code. I want to build a product that people actually use and appreciate, and that leaves a positive mark on the world.

### What I build

**[Meow Metrics®](https://meowmetrics.com)**, our main product, for managing community cat colonies under Spain's Animal Welfare Law 7/2023. I designed and built it end to end: architecture, data model, backend, front end and deployment on Azure (EU).

- Georeferenced map and census, with sterilisation status per colony and roll calls.
- Veterinary clinical records, outbreak map and official PDF certificates.
- Traceable alerts and actions, with cost, hours and version history.
- Roles and permissions, free accounts for volunteers, and an embeddable public map.
- Automatic monthly and scheduled reports, routines and work orders, and material inventory.
- A subsidy module that generates the application and its annexes in Word from the recorded data.
- Imports and integrations: ArcGIS, Excel, GeoJSON, KML/KMZ and GPX.
- Multilingual interface, in 12 languages.
- Automatic reading of documents (OCR), and **Ramón**, the mascot that explains each button.
- Training tool with micro-courses and diploma (in development).

**More products on the same platform**

- **Wild Metrics**: urban wildlife, starting with wild boar. Neighbour alerts, sensors, cameras and traps, risk forecasting, protocols and NGSI-LD interoperability.
- **Ark Metrics**: citizen participation (captures, fostering, adoption, donations). In pilot.
- **Silva Metrics**: living inventory of municipal green spaces. Prototype.
- **Census Metrics**: municipal pet census. Prototype.

### Data and AI

- Population model for colonies and a [free public calculator](https://meowmetrics.com/en/tnr-calculator/) built on it.
- City-level colony management plans generated from the recorded data and the applicable regulation.
- **GATINATOR**: individual cat recognition from photos.
- Open models running locally with Ollama, and our own models in training.

### Security and compliance

Hosting in the EU on Microsoft Azure, roles and permissions, versioned terms of use and privacy notices, and the Spanish National Security Scheme (ENS): basic-category declaration of conformity published, medium category in progress.

### Web, SEO and internal tooling

- Two multilingual WordPress/Elementor sites ([zoo-metrics.com](https://zoo-metrics.com) and [meowmetrics.com](https://meowmetrics.com)), with SEO and visibility in search engines and AI assistants (structured data, GA4, Search Console and Bing Webmaster).
- A marketing and sales system built with Claude agents: SEO/GEO, automated CRM, finance and KPI tracking, and content drafting with a human review step.
- Automated social posting for animal adoption pages through the Meta Graph API.
- Claude Code and the Claude API for internal development, code review and documentation (never embedded in the product).
- Working on a local tender-monitoring tool for public procurement.
