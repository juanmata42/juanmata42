### About me

I'm a full-stack developer from Spain. In 2024 I started [Zoometrics](https://zoo-metrics.com) with Diana Barrantes, a GovTech company that builds data infrastructure for managing urban biodiversity. City councils, associations, volunteers and vets use our tools to keep track of the animals they share their cities with.

Our main product is [Meow Metrics®](https://meowmetrics.com), a platform for managing community cat colonies. I designed and built all of it. As of 25 November 2026, 74 organisations (71 cities and 3 associations) in Spain, the United States, Greece and Bahrain use it, with 1,926 colonies, 16,168 cats and 38,123 events recorded. On the same platform we have **Wild Metrics**, **Ark Metrics** (in pilot) and prototypes of **Silva Metrics** and **Census Metrics**. If you want to see how it's going, take a look at [our website](https://zoo-metrics.com).

What I'm working on now: city-level colony management plans generated from the data, a population model with a [free calculator](https://meowmetrics.com/en/tnr-calculator/), automatic reading of forms and sheets, and GATINATOR, individual cat recognition from photos.

I'm still hands-on with product, architecture, data and code. I want to build a product that people actually use and appreciate, and that leaves a positive mark on the world.
