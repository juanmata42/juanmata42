### About me

I'm a full-stack developer from Spain. A few years ago I co-founded [Zoometrics](https://zoo-metrics.com), a GovTech company that builds data infrastructure for managing urban biodiversity. City councils, associations, volunteers and vets use our tools to keep track of the animals they share their cities with.

Our main product is [Meow Metrics®](https://meowmetrics.com), a platform for managing community cat colonies that's already used by municipalities in several countries. On the same platform we're building **Wild Metrics**, **Ark Metrics**, **Census Metrics** and **Green Metrics**. If you want to see how it's going, take a look at [our website](https://zoo-metrics.com).

I'm still hands-on with product, architecture, data and code. I want to build a product that people actually use and appreciate, and that leaves a positive mark on the world.
